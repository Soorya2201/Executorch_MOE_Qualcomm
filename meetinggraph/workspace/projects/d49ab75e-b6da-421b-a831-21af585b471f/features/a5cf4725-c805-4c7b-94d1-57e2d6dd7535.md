# Cytokinesis

## What it is
Cytokinesis is a phase that follows mitosis where the cytoplasm and organelles are divided between two daughter cells.

## Key points
- Cytokinesis occurs after mitosis to physically divide the cell into two daughter cells
- It involves the formation of the cleavage furrow, which pinches the cell in half
- The process is essential for ensuring that each daughter cell receives a complete set of organelles and cytoplasm
