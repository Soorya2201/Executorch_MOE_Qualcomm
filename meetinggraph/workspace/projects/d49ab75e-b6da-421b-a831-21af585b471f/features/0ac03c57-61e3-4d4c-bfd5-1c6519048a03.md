# DNA

## What it is
Deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) is a molecule that contains all the instructions for making proteins in cells. It has nucleotides made up of sugar, nitrogen base, and phosphate groups.

## Key points
- Shared genetic material among all cells
- Found in both prokaryotes and eukaryotes
- DNA contains all the instructions for making proteins in cells
- DNA is composed of nucleotides with three parts: sugar, nitrogen base (A, T, C, G), and a phosphate group
- Deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) has a double helix structure
