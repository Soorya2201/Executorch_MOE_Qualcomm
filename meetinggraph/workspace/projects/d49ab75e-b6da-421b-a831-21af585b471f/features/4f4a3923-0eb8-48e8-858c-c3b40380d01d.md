# Mitosis

## What it is
Mitosis is a process where a cell divides into two identical daughter cells. It involves several stages including prophase, metaphase, anaphase, and telophase.

## Key points
- Mitosis is the process of cell division in eukaryotic cells
- It results in two genetically identical daughter cells from one parent cell
- The process includes phases such as prophase, metaphase, anaphase, and telophase
- Mitosis involves the condensation and alignment of chromosomes in the middle of the cell.
- It results in two nuclei within one cell during telophase.
- Cytokinesis follows mitosis to divide the cytoplasm into two new cells.
- Prokaryotes do not undergo mitosis, while eukaryotic cells do.
- Mitosis is crucial for growth, development, and repair of tissues in multicellular organisms.
